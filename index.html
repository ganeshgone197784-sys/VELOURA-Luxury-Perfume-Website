The Maison

A House Built on Restraint

VELOURA was founded on a simple belief: that true luxury whispers. Far from the noise of mass fragrance, our perfumers work in small batches from an atelier in Kalyan, sourcing rare absolutes and aging every accord until it speaks with clarity. No shortcuts, no synthetics standing in for substance — only scent, considered as an art form.
