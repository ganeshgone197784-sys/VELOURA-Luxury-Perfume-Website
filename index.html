The Maison

Three generations of perfumers, one obsession with beauty.

VELOURA began in a small atelier where a single rule still governs every formula: nothing enters a bottle unless it is worth remembering. Our perfumers travel to source absolutes and resins at their origin — Bulgarian rose fields at dawn, oud forests of Assam, orange groves along the Mediterranean coast.

Each fragrance is macerated for months, never rushed, and finished by hand in crystal glass. The result is a wardrobe of scents built not for the moment, but for the memory it becomes.
